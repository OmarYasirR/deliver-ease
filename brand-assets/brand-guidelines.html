Brand guidelines · v1.0

DeliverEase — logistics, made easy.

A pin that confirms delivery. Blue for trust and movement, amber for the moment a parcel arrives. Every asset works in English and Arabic.

Logo

The primary lockup pairs the app icon with a two-weight wordmark: Poppins Bold for “Deliver”, Poppins Medium for “Ease”.

Primary — on white / light surfaces

Reversed — on Deep Navy / dark surfaces

Stacked — square and centred layouts

Arabic lockup — mirrored, icon on the right

Icon

Use the badge as the app icon, favicon and avatar. The single-colour version is for stamps, receipts and one-colour print.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the height of the check circle on every side. Never show the horizontal logo narrower than 120 px, or the icon smaller than 16 px.

Usage

Do

  • Use the reversed logo on dark backgrounds
  • Keep the amber check as the only warm colour
  • Mirror the lockup for Arabic layouts

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate or recolour the badge
  • Place the primary logo on busy photos
  • Add shadows, outlines or effects
